Hindi sila ang dapat katakutan

Sa Sa tinagal-tagal ko sa mundong ito, aking napagtanto ang isang totoo— hindi pala ang mababagsik na hayop ang dapat katakutan, kundi ang pusong sa awa ay tuluyang pinabayaan.

Sa Sa tinagal-tagal ko sa mundong ito, aking napagtanto ang isang totoo— hindi pala ang mababagsik na hayop ang dapat katakutan, kundi ang pusong sa awa ay tuluyang pinabayaan. Ang mga nilikhang walang laban, na buhay lamang ang siyang ipinaglalaban, ay walang habas na kinikitilan ng mga kamay na marunong mag-isip— ngunit piniling maging malamig at manhid. Hindi ba’t tayo ang may isip na tuwid? Tayo ang dapat umunawa at umalalay? Bakit tila baliktad ang daigdig— ang tao ang nananakit, at ang hayop ang napipilitang lumaban? Marahil kaya sila nagiging mabagsik ay dahil sa sugat na tayo rin ang may gawa. Sapagkat kahit sino’y lalaban kung una siyang sinaktan, kung paulit-ulit siyang tinapakan. Nakakaiyak masdan ang mga matang walang kamuwang-muwang sa kalupitan, mga pusong hindi marunong manakit ngunit tinuturuan ng mundo kung paano matakot sa tao. At ang pinakamasakit— may ilan na pumapatay hindi dahil sa pangangailangan, kundi dahil sa aliw, sa panandaliang libangan. Nawa’y pagpalain ng Diyos ang mga kamay na kumakalinga, ang mga pusong marunong umintindi. Sapagkat ang mga hayop ay dapat minamahal— hindi sinasaktan, hindi pinandidirihan, kundi pinoprotektahan, sa mundong tayo ang may kakayahang mag-isip nang tuwid.

You Rise

When shadows fall and strength feels thin, Remember — every loss can win. The road may twist, the night may stay, But dawn will always find its way.

When shadows fall and strength feels thin, Remember — every loss can win. The road may twist, the night may stay, But dawn will always find its way. Your heart was built to stand the storm, To break the mold, to shift, transform. Each scar you bear, each tear you’ve cried, Becomes the proof you’ve tried — survived. So lift your chin, breathe deep, begin, The world still waits for you to win. No mountain is tall when hope’s alive — You fall, you learn… and then you rise.

Unwritten Tale

She has no plans—her life’s a mystery, A wanderer in her own story. Through daily hues and starry skies, She follows dreams where freedom lies.

She has no plans—her life’s a mystery, A wanderer in her own story. Through daily hues and starry skies, She follows dreams where freedom lies. No map to mark, no path to trace, No fixed steps—just endless space. In raindrops' fall and morning’s grace, She learns to find her sacred place. No rules to bind, no strict design, She moves with time, her heart the sign. No plans—but hope there is all she’ll do, For faith will make her dreams come true. No plans—just living, day by day, In every choice along her way. Her tale is unwritten, wild, and free, No plans—just life’s pure melody.

Aklat

isa akong aklat— maaaring marikit ang pabalat, o pahinang nilaspag ng nagdaang panahon at hirap, ngunit bawat dahon ay may lihim na hindi mabigkas.

isa akong aklat— maaaring marikit ang pabalat, o pahinang nilaspag ng nagdaang panahon at hirap, ngunit bawat dahon ay may lihim na hindi mabigkas. ako’y malalim—kalipunan ng talinghaga’t pangarap, mga kabanatang tahimik na tanging hangin ang kausap, may mga tuldok na marahang tumutulo’t kumikislap— luha ng mga salitang kailanma’y di naipahayag. kung ako’y iyong bubuksan, handa ka bang lumingap sa mga pahinang nababalot ng dilim at ulap? ang tinta ng sugat na pilit kong hinaharap, mga salaysay sa pagitan ng paghinga at pagliyag. sapagkat ako rin ang may-akda ng sariling aklat, bawat taludtod at pahina’y alay sa nakalipas, sa mga matang sumulyap ngunit di nagtagal—agad, mga bumasa sa pabalat ngunit di umunawa nang sapat. at kung muli mo akong matagpuan sa iyong paglakad, huwag mo akong basahin upang unawain nang ganap, basahin mo ako upang damhin ang bawat pagliyab— sapagkat sa bawat salita’y may pusong naghihilom nang tapat. isa akong aklat na sinusulat ng bawat bukas, bawat pahina’y unti-unting bumabangon sa hirap, at sa dulo ng lahat ng aking pinagdaanan at pagliyag— natututo akong maging ako, kahit walang mambabasa’t tumatanggap.

Maybe Tomorrow

Maybe tomorrow will be different, the sky might open a gentler hue, and the weight I carry in silence...

Maybe tomorrow will be different, the sky might open a gentler hue, and the weight I carry in silence may feel a little lighter, too. Maybe tomorrow will be softer, the wounds are less sharp, the nights less long. Perhaps the echoes of my sorrow will turn into a quieter song. Maybe tomorrow will be kinder, a hand to hold, a chance to mend. For even if today feels endless, tomorrow waits—just 'round the bend.

I can’t focus

I can’t focus— the world feels too loud, and my thoughts keep running in circles that never close...

I can’t focus— the world feels too loud, and my thoughts keep running in circles that never close. I stare at the same spot and forget what I was thinking of. My heart beats too fast for a reason I can’t name. It’s like I’m trapped inside my head, and everything I feel is pressing against the walls, begging to be quiet— but it never ends. Sometimes I just want to disappear, not to die, but to rest from being me for a while. To stop pretending I’m fine, to stop carrying what no one seems to see. I want to escape this reality, just long enough to remember who I was

In Another Life

Maybe in another life, I’d have it all clear, No weight in my chest, no voice full of fear. Maybe I’d wake up with a purpose so bright...

Maybe in another life, I’d have it all clear, No weight in my chest, no voice full of fear. Maybe I’d wake up with a purpose so bright, Not lost in the shadows, but chasing the light. Maybe I wouldn’t feel so behind, Comparing my pace to others’ climb. Maybe I’d walk in the halls without shame, Not hiding my silence, not hiding my name. Maybe I’d study with joy, not dread, No thoughts of failure stuck in my head. Maybe I’d eat with a calm, steady heart, Not saving coins just to play my part. Maybe I’d laugh without guilt or pretend, Maybe I’d trust that this pain will end. Maybe I’d stand in a world that feels fair, Where dreams are allowed, and someone would care. Maybe I’d live in a small, quiet home, With a garden, a pond, a library of my own. Spoiling my parents with all that they missed, Giving them peace, giving them bliss. Maybe I’d be a vet, or fire fighter with pride, Travel the world with my loved ones beside. Maybe I’d never feel this loss in the night— Just a girl with a dream, and a heart full of light. But right now, I’m here—unsure and small, Trying to breathe, trying not to fall. Still hoping, still dreaming, still putting up a fight... Maybe not now— But maybe... in another life.

Fears

I’m scared of my future, like walking in a fog with no map in my hands. I study and I try...

I’m scared of my future, like walking in a fog with no map in my hands. I study and I try, but sometimes the lessons slip through my mind like sand through open fingers. And I wonder if something is in me is falling behind. I imagine myself someday, looking for a job, trying to stand strong while my confidence shakes, and my heart whispers what if I’m not enough? I think of my parents— their quiet sacrifices, their hands are tired for years of holding our world together. And the fear hits me hard: that I might become a burden, a disappointment, a weight they never deserved.

Wealth in Care

I want to be rich — not for my own name, Not for the glory, nor worldly fame. But for the chance to give and share, To show the world I truly care...

I want to be rich — not for my own name, Not for the glory, nor worldly fame. But for the chance to give and share, To show the world I truly care. I want my family to finally rest, To see them smile, to feel them blessed. My parents spent their lives in pain, Working hard through sun and rain. They’ve carried burdens far too long, They deserve peace — a gentle song. I dream of days without their tears, Of laughter echoing through the years. And then the strays — the lost, the small, Those with no home or love at all. I see them cold beneath the rain, And my heart aches with quiet pain. Someday I’ll build a shelter wide, Where every paw can run and hide. Where food and warmth will always stay, And love will chase their fears away. If I could help now, I truly would, I’d give my all, I really should. But for now, I stand where I am, With empty hands but a caring plan. Though I can’t give much today, I lift them all in earnest prayer. I pray for strength, for better days, For open hearts and brighter ways. To be the hands that give and feed, To live with love, not pride or greed. And though my wallet may be bare, My heart still finds its wealth in care. So I’ll keep dreaming, come what may, I’ll keep believing — I’ll find a way. For kindness grows where faith will stay, And till that time… For now, I pray.

To the Girl I Was

I know you're tired from trying to be strong, Smiling when it hurts, pretending you belong. You’re quiet when you're scared, loud when you're not okay...

I know you're tired from trying to be strong, Smiling when it hurts, pretending you belong. You’re quiet when you're scared, loud when you're not okay, Just hoping someone hears the words you never say. I want you to know, It’s okay to cry when no one's near, Okay to feel too much, to hold on to fear. You don’t have to be perfect, or always bright— Even stars need darkness to show their light. You’ll walk alone sometimes, Carrying dreams that feel too big, Fighting battles no one can dig. But trust me— You’ll survive every tear, every night you are shook, Because your strength lives in the pages no one ever took. One day, You’ll grow into a girl who still feels small, But stands anyway, even after every fall. And that’s something to be proud of.

Unfolding Story

My story’s pages still unfold, with ink that shimmers, soft and bold. Each turn, a chance, a gentle start — a new horizon in my heart.

My story’s pages still unfold, with ink that shimmers, soft and bold. Each turn, a chance, a gentle start — a new horizon in my heart. I do not rush, I do not know, where every fleeting step will go. But in the dance of time and chance, I find my peace in life’s romance.
